# Plane Alerts v4.6.0 — Prediction Intelligence & Instant Interaction

Plane Alerts v4.6.0 improves prediction confidence, uncertainty handling, historical-route evidence and Telegram interaction latency while keeping the established deterministic CPA engine authoritative.

## Prediction confidence and uncertainty

- Adds an evidence-based High / Medium / Low / Uncertain confidence layer.
- Confidence considers observation freshness/count/cadence, heading and speed stability, turn evidence, acceleration, distance trend, prediction horizon, missing fields and position uncertainty.
- Observation freshness is speed-dependent and bounded. Fast aircraft become uncertain sooner for the same position age than slow aircraft.
- Position uncertainty grows with speed, position age and irregular update timing rather than treating every stale observation equally.
- Ground-like motion is withheld from normal aerial pass prediction only when several observations support low-altitude, low-speed, non-climbing movement.

## Turn-aware shadow prediction

- The existing production trajectory/CPA predictor still controls alerts.
- A separate bounded turn-aware candidate runs in shadow mode for comparison.
- Stable turn evidence requires multiple fresh observations, consistent turn direction and bounded rate variance.
- One heading spike, stale history or short turn history cannot establish a turn.
- Candidate curvature decays into straight flight instead of being extrapolated indefinitely.
- Prediction Lab records production, linear-shadow and turn-shadow CPA/ETA evidence for later outcome scoring.

The turn-aware candidate is not promoted by this release. Missing ADS-B coverage remains unresolved rather than being scored as a successful or failed prediction.

## Route-history intelligence

- Historical route evidence now has explicit age decay instead of treating every stored route equally.
- Route retention is extended only to support bounded decayed evidence; old routes lose authority naturally.
- Similar historical paths are grouped in bounded background clustering outside the five-second critical loop.
- Small samples and contradictory clusters reduce historical confidence.
- Destination and old-route evidence are supporting uncertainty signals; they do not override direct physical evidence of a genuine pass.
- Terminal-area context continues to require stronger confirmation without treating destination=IST as automatic suppression.

## ETA and cancellation behavior

Existing ETA/cancellation safeguards remain in place: provider teleports are rejected, isolated heading spikes cannot replace a stable trajectory, and cancellation still requires repeated credible evidence. v4.6 does not add aggressive ETA smoothing because a stable but wrong ETA would be worse than a visible uncertainty change.

The v4.4 direct-presence recovery remains authoritative for fresh physical entry into the configured radius. Stale or repeated samples cannot resurrect a cancelled encounter.

## Telegram interaction latency

- `/next60` now acknowledges callback queries before forecast database work.
- Profile session reads use a bounded cache only after state has been successfully persisted.
- Duplicate callback IDs are handled idempotently.
- Interaction telemetry records handler-side callback acknowledgement, handler completion and message-completion distributions with p50/p95/p99/worst values.
- Telegram/network latency is kept separate from Plane Alerts handler latency.

## Observability

Prediction Lab diagnostics identify the production predictor version, confidence evidence, position freshness/uncertainty, linear and turn-aware shadow results and turn evidence. Route/terminal diagnostics remain available without logging unnecessary precise user-location data or secrets.

## Known limitations

- Turn-aware prediction remains shadow-only until replay and real production outcomes justify promotion in a later release.
- Confidence scores are deterministic diagnostic values, not calibrated probabilities; user-facing output remains categorical.
- Historical route clusters are intentionally simple and bounded. They are supporting evidence, not a learned flight-route model.
- Terminal-area uncertainty is modeled conservatively; runway and full airport/runway intelligence remain outside v4.6.
- Handler-side Telegram latency can be reduced and measured, but Telegram/client/network transport time is outside Plane Alerts control.
- Physical local ADS-B receiver quality still depends on the user's receiver/network environment.
