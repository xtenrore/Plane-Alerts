# Plane Alerts v5.1.0

Plane Alerts v5.1 extends the deterministic physical predictor from horizontal-only relevance to uncertainty-aware three-dimensional proximity while preserving horizontal CPA as a separate fallback and diagnostic value.

## Included

- vertical separation from the observer when observer elevation is known
- true three-dimensional/slant CPA and time to 3D CPA
- horizontal separation and vertical separation at the 3D closest point
- horizontal CPA retained separately and never hidden
- configurable altitude relevance through `proximity_3d.altitude_relevance`
- physical altitude and vertical-rate plausibility checks
- recent altitude continuity checks to reject provider/unit discontinuities
- conservative altitude uncertainty bounds
- optional observer terrain elevation backfill using the existing free Open-Meteo integration
- Prediction Lab diagnostics for horizontal CPA, 3D CPA, conservative 3D lower bound, altitude confidence and whether altitude relevance changed qualification
- dedicated v5.1 geometry/lifecycle regressions and deterministic geometry benchmark

## Safety and fallback behavior

Altitude is allowed to suppress a horizontally qualifying approach only when altitude evidence is trustworthy and the conservative 3D lower bound remains outside the configured radius. Missing, stale, malformed, discontinuous or otherwise uncertain altitude fails open to the existing horizontal prediction instead of suppressing an alert.

Observer elevation lookup is optional work. It is queued through the existing bounded enrichment worker and is never awaited on the five-second physical prediction path. Until observer elevation is known, v5.1 uses a conservative global terrain envelope; ambiguous cases retain the horizontal result.

Runtime AI and AGY have no authority over trajectory, CPA, 3D CPA, ETA, pass/no-pass, qualification, cancellation or alert timing.

Prediction version: `5.1-3d-proximity`.

## Observed-pass lifecycle correction

v5.1 also fixes an independently verified lifecycle ambiguity exposed while reviewing AGY cancellation findings. A close **projected** CPA is not treated as an observed pass. An active encounter is finalized as `passed` only after Plane Alerts has actually observed the aircraft inside the configured radius and a later fresh observation shows it receding from that observed closest point.

This preserves normal cancellation behavior for aircraft that merely looked likely to pass nearby but turned away before entering the radius, while preventing a genuinely observed pass from later being reported as a cancellation because route or CPA evidence changed.

Missing or stale ADS-B data never finalizes a pass.

## AGY findings reviewed during this release

- AGY seq 91 reported a roughly 0.64-second systematic late ETA bias. No ETA offset was applied because the cited Prediction Lab/lifecycle outcomes are explicitly non-scoreable and their sampling resolution is not sufficient to establish a sub-second correction. This remains **needs evidence** rather than a verified physical-predictor bug.
- AGY seq 92 reported cancellations with a roughly 6.37 km value. Production reconstruction demonstrated that at least one comparable case had a ~6.32 km **projected** CPA while the closest live observation remained around 18–24 km. v5.1 therefore does not treat projected CPA as ground truth.
- AGY seq 93 concerns sparse 30–60-minute evaluation coverage. That remains unresolved/unscoreable and belongs to the v5.2 shadow-model/evaluation roadmap rather than the v5.1 physical-geometry release.
- AGY seq 94 identified several encounters with cancel-state evidence despite in-radius observations. This was independently checked against production logs. `5001dc` had already completed correctly as `approach_passed` after an observed ~7.53 km minimum, but `06a37c` was cancelled after an observed ~8.04 km minimum and `3ccf0f` was cancelled after an observed ~11.30 km minimum. These are concrete evidence for the v5.1 observed-entry lifecycle correction and are preserved as regression cases.
- AGY seq 95 claims very early ETAs for two encounters whose later `passed_update` was 50–238 minutes away. The currently available evidence does not establish that those records belong to one continuous encounter rather than separate ICAO/callsign lifecycle windows or an audit join across stale history. No holding-pattern or ETA behavior was changed from that finding without a reproducible encounter replay.
- AGY seq 96 concerns unresolved/lost coverage in 30–60-minute shadow evaluation. Plane Alerts already treats missing ADS-B coverage as unresolved rather than success or miss; explicit long-horizon coverage-state telemetry belongs to the v5.2 evaluation release and is not used to change live v5.1 qualification.

## Release validation

The v5.1 release gate includes low-altitude nearby passes, directly overhead high-altitude passes, crossing tracks, climb/descent geometry, missing altitude, malformed/discontinuous altitude, unknown observer elevation, disabled altitude relevance, observed-pass versus cancellation behavior, the independently verified AGY seq94 encounter cases, all inherited Error Museum/terminal/storage/provider regressions, self-hosting builds, amd64 and ARM64 Docker validation, and a deterministic 3D geometry performance benchmark.

## Known limits

- Terrain elevation may be unavailable initially or during optional-provider failure. Horizontal CPA remains authoritative in ambiguous cases.
- ADS-B altitude is not treated as perfect geometric truth; uncertainty is explicitly carried into the suppression decision.
- Long-horizon 30–60-minute forecasts remain shadow-only and missing regional ADS-B coverage is not scored as success or failure.
